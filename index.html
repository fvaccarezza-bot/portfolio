(01) Who do I work with?

I work with startups, agencies, and companies of different sizes. Some clients need help with a single project, while others bring me in as an ongoing design partner.

(02) What kind of projects do I take on?

Mostly websites, product design, branding, presentations, and design systems. If it's design-related, there's a good chance I can help.

(03) How long does a project usually take?

It depends on the size of the project. Some are finished in a couple of weeks, while others take a few months. We'll define a timeline before getting started.

(04) Can I join an existing team?

Yes. I often work with founders, designers, developers, and agencies. I'm happy to adapt to the way your team already works.
